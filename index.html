REAL-DATA CASE STUDY / 02

Four business problems.
Evidence for each decision.

Each question is resolved analytically with a measured finding and a practical next action. The public data does not show whether the retailer deployed those actions.

01 / REVENUE RECONCILIATION

Which revenue number can finance trust?

Raw summation mixes positive sales, credits, duplicates, and non-merchandise adjustments. A typed ledger assigns every line once and reconciles the result.

£9.77mnet merchandise revenue from £10.25m gross less £475.9k credits

Decision enabled Use reconciled net merchandise revenue for product and market reviews. Audit excluded charges separately.

Inspect the reconciliation ↗
02 / CREDIT EXPOSURE

Where do credits distort performance?

A SKU can look exceptional in gross sales but contribute little after invoice cancellations. Both measures remain visible in the ranking.

£168.5kcredited on SKU 23843 against £168.5k gross sales

Decision enabled Investigate the large reversal before treating gross volume as repeatable demand.

Review credited products ↗
03 / ASSORTMENT PRIORITY

Which SKUs deserve review first?

A flat list of thousands of products hides where contribution is concentrated. Ranking net value puts high-impact products first.

831SKUs cover 80% of positive SKU net contribution

Decision enabled Prioritize availability and pricing reviews for high-contribution SKUs, then inspect low and negative net products.

Explore product ranking ↗
04 / CUSTOMER PRIORITIZATION

Which buyers call for different outreach?

Identified buyers are segmented by observed recency, frequency, and spend at a clear snapshot date.

96high-value buyers had no observed order for 90+ days at the full-history snapshot

Decision enabled Review that segment for potential reactivation, after checking current status and contact permissions.

02 / POLICY

Traceable classification

Exact duplicates are removed. Valid, positive-price merchandise becomes sales or cancellation credits. Other lines remain in an audit bucket.

Currency uses integer ten-thousandths of GBP. Product, monthly, and country totals reconcile to the ledger in all 12 views.
